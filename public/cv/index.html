Wireless power, optoelectronics
& scientific software

I’m a research engineer at ITMO University, combining physics, software, and electronics to develop working technology. My work spans wireless power and optoelectronics, from physical modelling and simulation to circuit design, embedded programming, prototypes, and measurement. I carry that work through to functioning devices and validate their performance under real operating conditions.

My experience includes published research on electric-vehicle and underwater wireless charging, engineering software for energy and circuit analysis, and audio DSP development. I use data processing and machine learning alongside hands-on testing to understand system behaviour, improve designs, and make research useful in practice.

Doctoral researcher in Physical and Mathematical Sciences
ITMO University · St. Petersburg, Russia

Circuit design

Matching Network Designer

L-match synthesis for resonant two-port networks, with reflected impedance, component losses, stress and bandwidth estimates, circuit schematics, and CSV export.

Wireless power design

WPT Unified Design Suite

A concept-design workspace combining coupled-coil estimates, power-stage efficiency predictors, and a saveable design summary for wireless power systems.

Open application ↗
Engineering estimation

Estimates coupling, inductances, Q factor, resistance, and efficiency for an 11 kW EV charging link using FEM-fitted polynomial models and a dimensioned preview.
