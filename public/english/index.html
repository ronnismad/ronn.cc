AI & prompts
Practical projects

A sample AI lesson: turn a vague request into a clear English prompt.

  1. 01

    Read

    Compare a vague prompt with a clear one. Find the goal, context, and requested format.

  2. 02

    Listen

    Listen to a partner describe what they need. Ask questions to fill in missing details.

  3. 03

    Speak

    Explain your prompt choices. Discuss what the AI should include and leave out.

  4. 04

    Write

    Write a prompt, review the answer, and improve one instruction.

↗
Something to take away

A reusable prompt and a short explanation of why each instruction helps.

Example prompt / Discuss, try, improve
Help me practise English for a job interview. I am an intermediate learner applying for a customer service role. Ask me one question at a time. After each answer, give one language correction and let me try again.

Clear goal → Useful context → Answer format → Review

These are sample lesson ideas, not a live AI tool. I adapt the language, pace, and support to the learner’s level and goals. Learners make the final choices and check AI suggestions.
